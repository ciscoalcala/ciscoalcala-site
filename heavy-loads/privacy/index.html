Heavy Loads Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

In short: Heavy Loads doesn't collect any information about you. Your settings stay on your device, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Information Heavy Loads stores

Heavy Loads saves the things you set up in the app, such as your plate inventory, your bars and collars, your preferred units, and your one-rep max. This information is stored only on your device so the app can remember your setup. It is never sent to us or to any server.

Information we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, or accounts
  • No health or fitness data, and no access to Apple Health
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No third-party services that receive your data

Apple diagnostics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This is controlled by you and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. These reports do not identify you.

Your choices

You can change or reset anything you've entered from within the app. Deleting Heavy Loads removes the app and everything it has stored from your device.

Children

Heavy Loads does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
