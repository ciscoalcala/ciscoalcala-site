Picale: Step Tracker

Your daily steps, dressed up in the colors of papel picado. Made for Apple Watch.

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Your steps at a glanceSee today's steps on your wrist, with an hour-by-hour chart of colorful flags.
  • Watch face complicationsPut your step count, goal progress, and daily chart right on your watch face.
  • Earn your banderitasSet a daily goal and earn a papel picado flag for every 1,000 steps. Hit your goal and the party starts.
  • Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your step data stays on your devices.
