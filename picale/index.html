Picale: Step Tracker

Your daily steps, dressed up in the colors of papel picado. Made for Apple Watch.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Your steps at a glancePicale reads your step count and shows it right on your wrist.
  • A festive designInspired by papel picado, the colorful cut-paper banners of Mexican celebrations.
  • Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your step data stays on your devices.

Requires Apple Watch. An iPhone app is not available yet.
