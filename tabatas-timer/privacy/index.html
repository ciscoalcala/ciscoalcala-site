Tabatas Timer Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

In short: Tabatas Timer uses Apple Health to show your heart rate during workouts and to save your workouts to the Health app. Everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We don't collect, store, sell, or share any of your information.

Health information Tabatas Timer uses

With your permission, Tabatas Timer uses Apple HealthKit on your iPhone and Apple Watch to:

  • Read your heart rate during a workout, so it can be displayed live on your Apple Watch and iPhone.
  • Save your workouts to Apple Health, including details such as the workout type, duration, and heart rate recorded during the session.

You choose which permissions to grant when the app first asks, and you can change them at any time.

How that information is used

Health data is used only to run your workouts, show your stats in the app, and save your workouts to the Health app. When you start a workout from your iPhone, workout information is passed directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch using Apple's built-in connection between the two devices. It is never sent to us or to any server.

Health data

  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining.
  • Health data is never sold, and never shared with third parties.
  • Health data is never stored by us in iCloud or on any server. Workouts you save to Apple Health are stored and synced by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Information we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, or accounts
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers and no ads
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No third-party services that receive your data

Apple diagnostics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This is controlled by you and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. These reports do not identify you.

Your choices

  • To change what Tabatas Timer can read or write, on your iPhone go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Tabatas Timer.
  • To view or delete workouts the app has saved, open the Health app, go to Browse > Activity > Workouts.
  • Deleting Tabatas Timer removes the app from your devices. Workouts it already saved stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Children

Tabatas Timer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
