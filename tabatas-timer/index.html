Tabatas Timer

A Tabata and interval timer for iPhone and Apple Watch. Start a workout from either device, watch your live heart rate, and save every session to Apple Health.

What it does

  • Tabata and interval timerWork and rest rounds with big, easy-to-read timer stats you can see mid-workout.
  • Start from iPhone or Apple WatchKick off a workout on your iPhone and it runs on your paired Apple Watch.
  • Live heart rateSee your heart rate on your wrist and on your iPhone as you train.
  • Works with Apple HealthYour finished workouts are saved to the Health app, alongside the rest of your activity.
  • Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your health data stays on your devices.

Workouts started from iPhone require a paired Apple Watch.
